Selling something

  1. From the homepage, tap Start selling.
  2. Fill in category, title, price (optional), neighborhood, and a description.
  3. Pay ₹20 to publish. Your listing goes live immediately.
  4. When a buyer pays for your item, they pay your full listed price plus a ₹20 platform fee — you receive the full price, the buyer covers the fee, not you.
  5. If your listing has no price (a job or a community post, say), a buyer pays ₹20 on its own to message you.

Finding something

  1. From the homepage, tap Find something and search, or browse a category from the results page.
  2. Use the filters (category, sort) to narrow things down.
  3. Open a listing to see the full description.
  4. Write a message. If the listing has a price, you pay the seller's price plus a ₹20 platform fee in one payment — the seller receives the listed price, we keep the ₹20. If there's no price, you pay ₹20 on its own to send the message.

Managing your postings

  1. After publishing, your listing is saved to this browser.
  2. The homepage shows a link to view them whenever you have active postings.
